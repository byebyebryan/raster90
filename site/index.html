The impossible schoolyard watch

The constraints are the character.

A small, earnest device trying to deliver more graphics and useful information than its fictional hardware should allow.

Time leads. Weather, date, steps, and battery stay concise. There are no seconds; ambient removes every non-time field.

A small palette, with a purpose.

Weather uses at most four visible colors. Battery tints are a separate status accent.

Authored pixels

The type and art come from exact matrices. Every lit cell has the same physical scale.

Truthful information

Missing weather gets a neutral icon and --. Stale data and unit conversion have explicit contracts.

Motion with an ending

Fresh weather gets one short gesture on Wear OS, then returns to its resting frame.
