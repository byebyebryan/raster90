## Evidence and history

- [Checkpoint index](checkpoints/README.md): dated build, emulator, physical,
  and owner-reported evidence.
- [Public media](media/README.md): the small publication subset and its exact
  provenance. Full evidence stays under ignored `outputs/`.
- [Design evolution](archive/design-evolution.md): calibration, rejected grid
  controls, earlier composition, and completed planning.
- [Concept artwork](../design/concepts/README.md): exploratory imagery.

Current references describe intended or implemented behavior. Validation
distinguishes source checks, simulated data, device captures, and wearer reports.
Archive documents preserve context and never override the current contract.
Keep operational commands in their runbook and link to them instead of copying
them into product notes or `AGENTS.md`.
